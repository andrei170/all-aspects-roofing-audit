The Page Race Is A Trap, And You Are Already In It
Somebody has sold this tactic to your whole market

JA Roofing publishes 5,941 pages built as /[service]/[town]. We pulled two at random — Axbridge and Crewkerne — and they share 97% of their unique vocabulary. It is one page with the town swapped, nearly six thousand times.

You have 402 doing the same thing. Neither of you built those by hand.

Here is why this matters for what you spend next. If somebody offers to grow your site to a thousand or five thousand pages, understand what you would be buying: more copies of a page Google already has. The firm with 5,941 has not solved anything — they have just spent more on the same bet.

The way out is not more pages. It is fewer, real ones — for the towns you actually work in, with your own jobs and photographs on them. Thirty genuine town pages will outperform four hundred copied ones, and they cost less to maintain.

What Nobody In This Market Is Doing
This is where the actual advantage is, and it is cheap
  • You are the only firm here with working analytics. JA Roofing has none. C.H. Roofing has none. LH Roofing has a Universal Analytics tag — that platform stopped processing data in July 2023, so it has collected nothing for over three years while presumably looking installed.
  • Nobody has a Meta pixel. Not one firm in this comparison. Whatever any of you spend on Facebook or Instagram is unmeasured and unretargetable.
  • C.H. Roofing publishes 4,691 words on its homepage and has no tappable phone number at all. The longest page in the market and no way to ring them from a mobile.
  • Bath & Wiltshire Roofing's website does not resolve. They appear in search results and the domain is dead.
  • You have proper structured data — organisation, opening hours, geographic coordinates, offers. We did not find that depth anywhere else here.

So the gap in this market is not content. It is that nobody can tell what is working. You are one call-tracking install away from being the only firm in Frome who knows which channel produces which job.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Whether LH Roofing's 54 pages are templated. We tested JA Roofing and yours; we did not test theirs.
  • Structured data on the three competitors. We read homepages for tags and phone links, not their full markup.
  • Everyone's Google Business Profile, reviews and map position, including yours. On local roofing that is where a great deal of enquiry begins and we have no reliable way to measure it.
  • What anybody spends on advertising, or what it returns, including you. The absence of a pixel tells us a tag is missing, nothing more.
  • Anyone's revenue, crew size, pricing or win rate, including yours.
  • Every roofer in Somerset and West Wiltshire. This is the set a search returned and we could verify — not a census.

Four firms, roughly six and a half thousand pages between them, and one working analytics tag. Yours.

The advantage here was never going to be page count.
